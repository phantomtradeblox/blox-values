🦅 About Eagle

A quick overview of Eagle's trading value, strengths, abilities, and uses.

📊 Family Data

  • Eagle is a paramecia type fruit

⚔️ Abilities

  • Eagle-themed aerial attacks.
  • Strong ranged pressure.
  • Excellent aerial mobility.
  • Strong positioning potential.
  • Useful for chasing opponents.

🟢 Pros

  • Strong mobility.
  • Good aerial pressure.
  • Useful ranged attacks.
  • Strong PvP potential.
  • Good movement options.

🔴 Cons

  • Requires good positioning.
  • Some attacks require accurate timing.
  • Aerial combat can require practice.
  • Mistimed attacks can leave openings.
  • Not the easiest fruit for every player.

🎯 Best For

  • Experienced PvP players.
  • Players who enjoy aerial combat.
  • Players who value mobility.
  • Ranged combat.
  • Players looking for Eagle variants.

🎯 Ranged Pressure
