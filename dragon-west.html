🐉 About Dragon

A quick overview of Dragon's trading value, strengths, abilities, and uses.

  • Perm Dragon value: 10B
  • Dragon West value: 4.1B
  • Dragon East value: 3.5B
  • Eclipse Dragon value: 7.5B

⚔️ Abilities

  • Powerful ranged Dragon attacks.
  • Large area-of-effect attacks.
  • Strong mobility and aerial pressure.
  • Dragon transformation provides a major combat boost.
  • Excellent pressure in PvP when abilities are chained correctly.

🟢 Pros

  • Extremely high trading value.
  • 10/10 demand for the standard Dragon versions.
  • Highly desirable Mythical fruit.
  • Strong trading power in high-value deals.
  • Strong PvP and grinding potential.

🔴 Cons

  • Extremely expensive compared with most fruits.
  • Standard Dragon has very low availability.
  • Its high value can make fair trades difficult to arrange.
  • Some abilities require good timing and aim.
  • Not the easiest fruit for every player to master.

🎯 Best For

  • High-value trading.
  • Experienced PvP players.
  • Players who want a powerful Mythical fruit.
  • End-game players with expensive inventories.
  • Collectors looking for valuable fruits.
