🥶 About Yeti

A quick overview of Yeti's trading value, strengths, abilities, and uses.

📊 Family Data

  • Yeti value: 140M.
  • Fiend Yeti value: 1.1B.
  • Plastic Yeti value: 70M.
  • Yeti has 8/10 demand.
  • Fiend Yeti has 9/10 demand.

⚔️ Abilities

  • Powerful ice-based attacks.
  • Large area-of-effect damage.
  • Strong transformation-based combat.
  • Excellent mobility while transformed.
  • Strong freezing and defensive potential.

🟢 Pros

  • High trading demand.
  • Powerful PvP potential.
  • Strong grinding capability.
  • Large attacks make it effective against groups.
  • Multiple versions give collectors more options.

🔴 Cons

  • Expensive compared with most fruits.
  • Some attacks require accurate timing.
  • Its transformation can take practice to use effectively.
  • High-value versions can be difficult to trade fairly.
  • Fiend Yeti is especially expensive compared with regular Yeti.

🎯 Best For

  • Experienced PvP players.
  • Grinding and PvE.
  • Players who like transformation-based fruits.
  • High-value trading.
  • Collectors looking for rare Yeti versions.

✨ Versions

  • Yeti
  • Fiend Yeti
  • Plastic Yeti

⚔️ Combat Modes

PvP: Yeti is suited for aggressive PvP thanks to its large attacks, freezing effects, mobility, and transformation-based pressure.
Grinding: Yeti's wide attacks and strong transformed form make it useful for clearing groups of enemies and progressing through PvE content.
Fiend Yeti: Fiend Yeti keeps the Yeti identity while providing a darker variant with altered visual effects and a more aggressive style.
Trading: Yeti versions are primarily valuable to players and collectors interested in high-demand Mythical fruits and limited variants.
