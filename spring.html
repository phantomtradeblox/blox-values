🌱 About Spring

A quick overview of Spring's trading value, abilities, strengths, and combat uses.

📊 Fruit Data

  • Spring is a common trading fruit.
  • It focuses heavily on movement and spring-based attacks.
  • The fruit is good for players who hate happyness.

⚔️ Abilities

  • Spring-based attacks.
  • Powerful bouncing movement.
  • Good mobility during combat.
  • Can quickly reposition around opponents.
  • Useful for aggressive close-range pressure.

🟢 Pros

  • Good mobility.
  • Fun movement-based combat.
  • Easy to understand.
  • Useful for aggressive positioning.
  • Affordable compared with higher-value fruits.

🔴 Cons

  • Low trading value compared with higher-tier fruits.
  • Limited range compared with stronger fruits.
  • Requires movement control to use effectively.
  • Damage can be lower than many stronger fruits.
  • Not ideal for every combat style.

🎯 Best For

  • Players who enjoy mobility.
  • Aggressive close-range combat.
  • Players learning movement.
  • Players looking for an inexpensive fruit.
  • Players who enjoy spring-based attacks.
