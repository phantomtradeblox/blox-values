🥊 About Rubber

A quick overview of Rubber's combat style, strengths, abilities, and uses.

📊 Fruit Data

  • Rubber is a Paramecia-type fruit.
  • Rubber focuses heavily on close-range and ranged combat.
  • The fruit provides strong resistance against several forms of damage.
  • Rubber is especially useful for players who enjoy aggressive combat.
  • Rubber does not have a Plastic variant.

⚔️ Abilities

  • Powerful rubber-based punches.
  • Long-range stretching attacks.
  • Strong area-of-effect attacks.
  • High mobility during certain attacks.
  • Effective combo potential.

🟢 Pros

  • Strong PvP potential.
  • Good range on several attacks.
  • Useful combo options.
  • Unique defensive properties.
  • Fun and aggressive combat style.

🔴 Cons

  • Requires good aim and timing.
  • Some attacks can be difficult to land.
  • Combo mistakes can leave the user open.
  • Not every ability is easy to use.
  • Requires practice to use effectively.

🎯 Best For

  • PvP players.
  • Aggressive players.
  • Combo-focused players.
  • Players who enjoy ranged attacks.
  • Players who want a unique combat style.

✨ Versions

  • Rubber

⚔️ Combat Modes

🥊 PvP

Strong choice for aggressive PvP, using Rubber's ranged attacks, combos, and pressure.

👊 Combo Combat

Effective when chaining Rubber's attacks together to keep opponents under pressure.

🎯 Ranged Combat

Several Rubber attacks can be used from a distance, allowing players to pressure opponents before engaging.
