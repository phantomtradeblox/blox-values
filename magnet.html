🧲 Magnetic Power System

Magnetic Power: Collecting metal scraps helps fill Magnet's power meter. As the meter increases, its mechanical arms grow and its combat abilities become stronger.
Magnetic Attraction: Magnet uses magnetic forces to pull nearby scraps and pressure opponents. The strength and reach of some effects depend on its Magnetic Power.
Mechanical Transformation: Magnet can transform into a large mech-style form with its own combat capabilities. The transformation is a major part of its fighting style.
Trading Reminder: Magnet's physical and permanent versions have different trading values. Always check the correct version and the latest value before accepting a trade.
