🐯 About Tiger

A quick overview of Tiger's trading value, strengths, abilities, and uses.

📊 Family Data

  • Tiger value: 160M.
  • Tiger demand: 8/10.
  • Werewolf value: 2.2B.
  • Werewolf demand: 9/10.
  • Plastic Tiger value: 550M.
  • Plastic Tiger demand: 6/10.

⚔️ Abilities

  • Powerful close-range combat pressure.
  • Strong mobility during combat.
  • High PvP potential when abilities are chained correctly.
  • Can apply strong pressure against opponents.
  • Works well for aggressive combat playstyles.

🟢 Pros

  • High trading value compared with most fruits.
  • Strong demand at 8/10.
  • Werewolf provides extremely high trading value.
  • Good choice for aggressive PvP players.
  • Multiple versions make the Tiger family collectible.

🔴 Cons

  • Standard Tiger has much lower value than Werewolf.
  • Plastic Tiger has lower demand than the standard version.
  • High-value versions can be difficult to trade fairly.
  • Some combat abilities require good timing and aim.
  • The strongest versions are expensive to obtain.

🎯 Best For

  • High-value trading.
  • Experienced PvP players.
  • Players who prefer aggressive combat.
  • End-game players with expensive inventories.
  • Collectors looking for Tiger family variants.

✨ Versions

  • Tiger
  • Werewolf
  • Plastic Tiger

⚔️ Combat Modes

PvP: Tiger is best suited to aggressive players who can consistently chain attacks and use its mobility effectively.
Grinding: Tiger can be used for grinding, especially by players who prefer active combat rather than passive farming.
Trading: Tiger is mainly valuable for trading, with Werewolf being the highest-value version in the listed Tiger family.
Collecting: The Tiger, Werewolf, and Plastic Tiger versions give collectors multiple forms of the same family to obtain.
